# Original Materials

Store unchanged copies of the source files or datasets used in your project here.

For each source or dataset, record:

- Title or short description.
- Creator, institution, or repository.
- Original URL or catalogue reference.
- Date accessed.
- License or conditions of use.
- Historical scope and unit of observation.
- Known limitations or omissions.

Do not edit these files. Create a copy in `../working/` before cleaning, normalizing, filtering, or restructuring the data.

Do not upload material that is confidential, personal, copyrighted, or otherwise restricted unless you have permission to do so.
