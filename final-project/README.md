# Final Project

Use this folder to document the complete workflow for your independent digital history project.

Your project should include:

- A historically meaningful research question.
- Clearly identified source material or data.
- Discussion of provenance, coverage, and significant absences.
- At least one substantial digital method introduced in the course.
- Documentation of important transformations and decisions.
- Validation against the underlying data or historical sources.
- Historically grounded interpretation.
- Explicit discussion of uncertainty and limitations.

Use the subfolders to separate original materials, transformed data, processing procedures, analytical outputs, validation, and final presentation files.

Do not wait until the end of the semester to organize this folder. Add materials and documentation as the project develops.
